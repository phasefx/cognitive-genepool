### 5. The Intervention: Multi-Class Moves
*The class that summoned the monster cannot defeat it. Change the party — with a concrete action.*

**To break a Reboot Loop:**
- **Paladin move:** Pick a finish line you can't renegotiate. Write it down. Tell someone.
- **Ranger move:** Get one external signal in the next 10 minutes (ask a human, run a tiny test, touch grass).

**To break a Metric Maximizer:**
- **Rogue move:** Find where the metric is being gamed. Name the gap between metric and goal.
- **Paladin move:** Re-anchor to the terminal goal. Say it out loud.

**To break a Captured Narrator:**
- **Ranger move:** Touch grass. Get an error signal from outside the narrative.
- **Wizard move:** Build one falsifiable prediction. Write down what would prove you wrong.

**To break an Ossified Scaffold:**
- **Rogue move:** Ask "what would we do if this tool didn't exist?"
- **Bard move:** Tell a new story about why the old scaffold served its purpose and can be retired.

### 6. Leveling Up: The Cost of Mastery
*Mastery isn't just "being better"; it's being willing to pay the price.*

| Class | Level 20 Mastery | The Price You Pay |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wizard** | Knows when to stop modeling. | Being called "pedantic" or "aloof." |
| **Ranger** | Trusts the signal, even when it's weird. | Being unable to "explain" your gut. |
| **Bard** | Uses story to steer, not to hide. | Being seen as "slippery" or "theatrical." |
| **Artificer** | Deletes scaffolds that stop working. | Being accused of "over-engineering." |
| **Paladin** | Holds terminal goals; flexes instrumental ones. | Being called "inflexible" or "mean." |
| **Rogue** | Questions the "obvious" without getting lost. | Being seen as "difficult" or "contrarian." |
