    ### 3. Stress Response: The Hero-to-Monster Pipeline

    | If the Lead is... | Stress Mode (Monster) | The Tell |

    ### 4. The Intervention: Multi-Classing
    *The class that summoned the monster cannot defeat it. You must change the party.*

    *   **To break a Reboot Loop:** Summon the **Paladin** (Commitment) and **Ranger** (External Signal).
    *   **To break a Metric Maximizer:** Summon the **Rogue** (Find the gaming) and **Paladin** (Re-anchor to Goal).
    *   **To break a Captured Narrator:** Summon the **Ranger** (Touch grass) and **Wizard** (Falsifiable Model).
    *   **To break an Ossified Scaffold:** Summon the **Rogue** (Question the tool) and **Bard** (New Story).

    ### 5. Leveling Up: The Cost of Mastery
    *Mastery isn't just "being better"; it's being willing to pay the price of that perspective.*

    | Class | Level 20 Mastery | The Price You Pay |
    | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | **Wizard** | Knows when to stop modeling. | Being called "pedantic" or "aloof." |
    | **Ranger** | Trusts the signal, even when it's weird. | Being unable to "explain" your gut. |
    | **Bard** | Uses story to steer, not to hide. | Being seen as "slippery" or "theatrical." |
    | **Artificer** | Deletes scaffolds that stop working. | Being accused of "over-engineering." |
    | **Paladin** | Holds terminal goals; flexes instrumental ones. | Being called "inflexible" or "mean." |
    | **Rogue** | Questions the "obvious" without getting lost. | Being seen as "difficult" or "contrarian." |
