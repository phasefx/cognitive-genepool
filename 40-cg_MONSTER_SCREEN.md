# 🐲 MONSTER / MONSTER PARTY SCREEN  
### *Tactical Overlay for Coordinated Pathologies*

**Use this screen ONLY when single-pattern diagnosis fails and interventions provoke compensation.**  
This is not a worldview. It is a **combat HUD**.

---

## ACTIVATION CONDITION (Flip This Screen When…)

☑ You addressed the behavior, but it adapted  
☑ Insight did not produce control  
☑ Fixing one thing made another worse  
☑ You feel *strategically opposed*, not merely confused  
☑ You hear: “I know this is bad, but…”  
☑ The same loop survives consequences

> **If you are asking “why doesn’t this work?” you are still on the DM Screen.**  
> **If you are asking “who’s covering whom?” you are here.**

---

## CORE RULE (Read First)

> **You are not fighting a monster.  
> You are fighting a *coordination*.**

Do not optimize for truth.  
Do not optimize for understanding.  
Optimize for **breaking synergy**.

---

## THE PARTY ID CARD (Fill This In Fast)

| Question | Identify |
|---------|---------|
| What repeats despite consequences? | **DPS** |
| What blocks exits or alternatives? | **Tank** |
| What explains why this is fine/necessary? | **Healer** |
| What damage accumulates over time? | **Controller** |
| What anticipates intervention? | **Scout** (if any) |
| How do they coordinate? | **Comms** |
| What happens if I hit one role? | **Compensation pattern** |

**If you can’t fill this in, you are not facing a party yet. Go back to the DM Screen.**

---

## ACTION ECONOMY REALITY CHECK

**You:**  
- How many fronts can I *actually* act on at once? ☐ 1 ☐ 2 ☐ 0.5  
- What resources do I control? (time, environment, authority, money, allies)

**The Party:**  
- How many fronts can it respond on simultaneously? ☐ 2 ☐ 3 ☐ All  
- What does it do *for free*? (craving, narrative, inertia)

> **If the party has more actions than you, brute force will fail.**

---

## ROLE INTERACTIONS (Know This Cold)

- **Attack DPS →** Healer rationalizes, Tank tightens, Controller spikes pain  
- **Attack Healer →** DPS surges, Tank blocks harder  
- **Attack Tank →** DPS escalates to cope  
- **Attack Controller →** Usually too late (damage already inscribed)

**This is not malice. It is coordination.**

---

## DISALLOWED MOVES (These FEED the Party)

❌ Arguing with the Healer  
❌ Pure willpower against DPS  
❌ Insight without environmental change  
❌ “Just one more explanation”  
❌ Waiting for the Controller to heal itself  
❌ Simultaneous attacks on all roles (you will lose)

If you feel *righteous*, *exhausted*, or *clever*, stop.  
You are probably feeding Comms.

---

## ALLOWED MOVES (Choose ONE Primary)

### 1. Silence the Healer
- Stop arguing  
- Use incoherence checks (“What would change your mind?”)  
- Introduce third-party reality anchors  
- Strategic silence  

**Goal:** Let damage be *felt*, not explained away

---

### 2. Banish or Replace the Tank
- Change location, access, routine, structure  
- Remove cues entirely  
- Substitute a new scaffold (program, community, rules)  

**Goal:** Expose DPS to direct intervention

---

### 3. Aggro Split
- External authority absorbs reaction (sponsor, institution, medical)  
- Shift conflict away from you  

**Goal:** Win the action economy

---

### 4. Role Displacement
- Replace a role, don’t destroy it  
- New Tank, new DPS, safer Healer narrative  

**Goal:** Preserve function, reduce damage

---

### 5. Bounded Catastrophe
- Create a controlled discontinuity  
- Crisis with limits, witnesses, exits  

**Goal:** Force regime change without terminal stakes

---

## ⚠️ TPK WARNING (DO NOT IGNORE)

If your next thought sounds like:

- “I’ll just go all in”  
- “Once it collapses, I’ll rebuild”  
- “I need to hit bottom”  
- “There’s nowhere to go but up”  

**This is not clarity.  
This is the party cornering the player.**

➡️ **Recruit help immediately**  
➡️ **Create a bounded catastrophe instead**  
➡️ **Do not roll this saving throw alone**  
➡️ *Other players may have Inspiration*

## EXIT CONDITIONS (Put the Screen Down When…)

☑ The party is split or desynced  
☑ One role is neutralized or replaced  
☑ Environmental pressure has changed  
☑ You can reason about a single pattern again  
☑ You feel tempted to keep fighting instead of rebuilding

> **Staying in combat mode after combat ends creates new monsters.**

---

## FINAL REMINDER

- Monster Parties form around **binding stress**  
- If the stress remains, the party will reform  
- Winning a fight ≠ dissolving the configuration  

After combat:  
➡️ Switch to **Integration Lens**  
➡️ Ask what the party was trying to solve  
➡️ Build a healthier configuration

---
