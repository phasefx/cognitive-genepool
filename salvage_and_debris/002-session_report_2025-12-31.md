## New Monster Entry: The Reboot Loop

*The Sisyphean Refactor, The Clean Slate Siren*

### COMBAT CARD

**Pattern:** A system that restarts projects at the point where synthesis becomes work, mistaking "starting fresh" for progress.

**Tell:** "If I just restructure this, it'll be easier to finish." (Said for the third time.)

**Why it matters:** The loop converts exploration energy into infinite preamble. Nothing ships. The work that *feels* like progress (reorganizing, reframing, restarting) is actually the avoidance of the work that *is* progress (finishing).

---

#### CORE DIMENSIONS

**Cognitive Lightcone (Care):**
- Scope: The project, the vision, the "someday" version
- Exclusions: The actual current state; the "good enough" threshold

**Goal Structure:**
- Terminal: Ship something real (ostensibly)
- Instrumental: Refactor, reorganize, restart (actually)
- Replacement tolerance: High for methods, zero for the fantasy of the perfect version

**Competency:**
- High at beginnings — ideation, structuring, fresh energy
- Low at middles — grinding, commitment, tolerating imperfection

**Intervention Sensitivity:**
- Goal-level: Low (the desire to ship is genuine; the avoidance is subterranean)
- Method-level: Moderate (can be redirected to smaller deliverables)
- Model-level: High (reframing "done" as "exists and is honest about incompleteness")

---

#### LAIR ACTIONS

- **Infinite Preamble:** Makes "preparation" feel like progress, extending runway indefinitely
- **Perfectionism Fog:** Obscures the difference between "unfinished" and "unshippable"
- **Novelty Injection:** Each restart brings fresh dopamine, reinforcing the loop

---

#### INTERVENTION EFFECTIVENESS

| Approach | Effectiveness | Why |
|:---------|:--------------|:----|
| "Just push through" | ☠️ Backfires | Increases aversion, triggers harder reboot impulse |
| Willpower/discipline framing | ❌ Ineffective | Misdiagnoses the mechanism; it's not laziness |
| Accountability partner | ⚠️ Risky | Can help, but can also become another thing to avoid |
| Ship something tiny | ✓✓ Critical | Makes "done" taste achievable; builds completion muscle |
| Reframe "done" as "v0.1" | ✓ Effective | Lowers the threshold; completion becomes iteration |
| Time-box the restart impulse | ✓ Effective | "You can reboot, but not until X ships" |

---

**Takeaway:** *The Reboot Loop isn't about the project being wrong. It's about completion being uncomfortable. Ship small until finishing feels survivable.*
